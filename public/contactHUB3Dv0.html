About HUB3D

HUB3D is a 3D printing design aggregator built to solve a simple problem: finding designs that actually work with your printer and materials. Instead of browsing four different sites and guessing whether a design is compatible with your setup, HUB3D lets you register your printers and filaments once and then filters results across MyMiniFactory, Cults3D, Thingiverse, and Printables automatically.

This project was built as a university final project, using Node.js, Express, Supabase, and Bootstrap.

FAQ

Yes — an account is required to use HUB3D. This lets me save your printer and filament setup so I can filter designs for you automatically. Registration is quick and requires only an email and password.

Add your printers and filaments from the Profile page. When you enable My Setup Only on the Designs page, HUB3D filters results to only show designs that are compatible with your registered equipment.

Designs are fetched from MyMiniFactory and Cults3D via their public APIs. Thingiverse and Printables designs are served from a curated static dataset. HUB3D does not host any design files — clicking a card takes you directly to the original source.

Yes, HUB3D is completely free. This is a university project built for educational purposes and is not intended for commercial use.
